Search engine optimisation

Build visibility that
works over time.

Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the process of improving a website so that relevant people can discover it through organic search. It brings together technical health, useful content, clear site structure and a good page experience.

Our approach starts with understanding your services, customers and search landscape. From there, we prioritise practical improvements rather than chasing rankings for keywords that do not support your business.

What SEO can include

Four connected parts
of search performance.

01

SEO audit & technical SEO

Review crawlability, indexation, site structure, page performance, redirects and technical issues that may affect search visibility.

02

On-page SEO

Improve page titles, headings, internal links, content structure and search intent alignment on important pages.

03

SEO content strategy

Plan helpful, original content around the questions, needs and search journeys of your target audience.

04

Measurement & reporting

Use tools such as Google Search Console and analytics to understand impressions, clicks, queries and meaningful actions.

SEO questions

Good questions to ask
before starting SEO.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is not instant. Timing depends on the website, competition, technical condition, content quality and the work being implemented. We avoid guaranteeing a specific ranking or timeline.

Can you guarantee the number-one position on Google?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a particular Google ranking. We focus on sound optimisation, useful content, transparent measurement and improvements that support your business goals.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. An SEO project can begin with an audit of your existing website, followed by a prioritised plan for technical, content and on-page improvements.

Do I need SEO if I already use social media?

Social media and SEO serve different discovery journeys. Social platforms help you reach audiences in feeds, while SEO helps people find relevant pages when they search.
