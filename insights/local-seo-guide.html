Local SEO: help nearby customers discover your business

Local SEO helps businesses appear when people search for products or services in a particular area. Strong local visibility starts with accurate information and a useful experience for customers.

By Bashademy•2026-11-27

Keep your business information accurate

Make sure your business name, contact details, website and service information are accurate wherever customers encounter them. If your business is eligible for a Google Business Profile, claim and maintain it using the real-world details that represent your business.

Choose categories and describe your services clearly

Use the most accurate business category available and describe the services you genuinely provide. Avoid adding unrelated categories or location keywords that do not represent the business.

Create useful location information on your website

If you serve particular areas, explain the services available there and what customers in that area need to know. Create a location page only when it offers distinct, useful information — not simply to swap one place name for another.

Make reviews part of your customer experience

Invite genuine customers to share honest feedback and respond professionally. Never buy reviews, offer rewards in exchange for positive reviews or write reviews for your own business.

Build local relevance beyond your website

Participate in relevant local communities, partnerships and industry activities. Ensure that business information shared on trusted directories and partner websites is consistent and accurate.

Measure local discovery

Review the performance data available in your Business Profile and Search Console. Look at discovery queries, website visits, calls and direction requests where those metrics are available. Use the information to improve the customer journey.

A practical next step

Review your current website and choose one improvement you can implement and measure. Consistent, useful improvements are more valuable than chasing quick fixes.
